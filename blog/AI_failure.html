AI promises change, but why do so many pharmaceutical projects fail before they even start?

AI promises change, but why do so many pharmaceutical projects fail before they even start?

It's not about budgets or technology.

It's about misunderstandings. Here are my top 5 reasons AI projects flop in our industry:

  • No clear business problem. Too many teams chase AI for AI's sake, without defining what it should actually solve.

  • Data chaos. Pharma sits on mountains of data, but much of it is messy, siloed, or inaccessible. Without solid data foundations, AI is doomed from the start.

  • Siloed ownership. When IT or data science teams run the show in isolation, commercial and medical leaders are left out. Without cross-functional buy-in, projects stall.

  • Change resistance. Even brilliant AI solutions get ignored if people don't trust or understand them. Culture eats algorithms for breakfast.

  • Chasing hype, skipping basics. Everyone wants the next big thing...now, but skipping core processes and governance means initiatives unravel fast.

    • If these sound familiar, you're not alone.

    The truth: AI isn't magic. It's just another tool, and it only works when you fix the real business pains, build strong teams, and put basics before buzzwords.
