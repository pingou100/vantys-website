Healthcare Pathway Transformation:
Proactive Cardiovascular Risk Management

Healthcare System Partnership Spain Cardiovascular 18 months European Biotech

The Transformation Pathway

The Challenge

Transitioning from reactive treatment to proactive risk management

An emerging European biotech company sought to establish a value-based partnership with a Spanish regional health system to transition from reactive cardiovascular disease treatment to proactive risk management.

The health system faced rising hospitalisation costs and delayed diagnosis of high-risk patients, while the biotech needed to demonstrate real-world evidence of their innovative cardiovascular therapies in a preventive care model.

Our Approach

A data-driven population health management programme

We designed and implemented a programme integrating multiple digital and clinical touchpoints:

  • Risk Stratification EngineDeployed machine learning algorithms across regional health datasets to identify patients with elevated cardiovascular risk profiles based on biomarkers, comorbidities, and social determinants of health.
  • Omnichannel Patient EngagementEstablished a coordinated call centre and digital platform to increase disease awareness, facilitate biomarker screening, and guide patients through the cardiovascular risk assessment pathway.
  • Remote Monitoring InfrastructureProvisioned connected devices and patient education kits to enable continuous monitoring of key cardiovascular metrics, supported by clinical protocols for escalation and treatment optimisation.
  • Pathway RedesignReengineered the care pathway from diagnosis through treatment, integrating the biotech's novel therapies as part of guideline-concordant care for identified high-risk patients.
The Outcome

Measurable improvements within 18 months

+18%Increase in identification of at-risk patients vs standard-of-care pathways
−7%Reduction in cardiovascular-related hospitalisations within the targeted population
18moSustainable partnership model established demonstrating value to all stakeholders
  • 18% increase in identification of patients at risk of cardiovascular events compared to standard-of-care pathways.
  • 7% reduction in cardiovascular-related hospitalisations within the targeted population.
  • Established a sustainable partnership model demonstrating value to payers, clinicians, and patients — supporting expanded market access for the biotech's portfolio.
