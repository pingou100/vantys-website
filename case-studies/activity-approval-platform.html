The Transformation Pathway

The Challenge

Breaking through operational bottlenecks in HCP engagement

A mid-size pharmaceutical company with operations across 15 European markets faced significant frustration with their HCP engagement approval process. Medical affairs teams spent days preparing activity requests, only to face weeks of back-and-forth iterations with compliance, legal, and finance.

The manual process created delays that meant missed opportunities for timely scientific exchange, frustrated field teams who felt disconnected from decision-making, and compliance teams overwhelmed by fragmented documentation across email chains and spreadsheets. The organization needed a solution that would preserve necessary controls while dramatically reducing friction.

Our Approach

A user-centric platform prioritizing efficiency and transparency

Rather than implementing a heavy enterprise system, we designed a streamlined solution focused on eliminating waste in the approval journey:

  • Process ReengineeringMapped and streamlined end-to-end workflows across all 15 markets, removing redundant approval steps while maintaining necessary controls. Created standardized templates that captured 80% of required information upfront, eliminating multiple rounds of clarification.
  • Open-Source Technology StackDeployed a modern, open-source platform that automated routing, notifications, and documentation. The system integrated seamlessly with the organisation's existing HCP payment systems and compliance databases, eliminating manual data re-entry and reducing errors.
  • User Experience FocusDesigned interfaces that field teams could navigate intuitively without extensive training. Progressive disclosure ensured users only saw fields relevant to their activity type, while built-in validation caught common errors immediately rather than after approval submission.
  • Embedded Compliance & EfficiencyBuilt compliance rules directly into the workflow engine, automatically routing activities to appropriate reviewers based on activity type, budget, and country regulations. Real-time dashboards gave all stakeholders visibility into approval status, eliminating status inquiries.
