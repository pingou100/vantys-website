Operational Excellence: HCP Engagement Activity Approval Platform

Business Operations TransformationComplianceopen-sourceField-based employees efficiency

The Transformation Pathway

The Challenge

Breaking through operational bottlenecks in HCP engagement

A biotech company with growing operations across 10 European markets faced significant frustration with their HCP engagement approval process. Medical affairs and Commercial Teams spent days processing activity requests, only to face weeks of back-and-forth iterations with compliance, legal, and finance.

The manual process created delays that meant missed opportunities for timely scientific exchange, frustrated field teams who felt disconnected from decision-making, and compliance teams overwhelmed by fragmented documentation across email chains and spreadsheets. The organization needed a solution that would preserve necessary controls while dramatically reducing friction.

Our Approach

A user-centric platform prioritizing efficiency and transparency

Rather than implementing a heavy enterprise system, we designed a streamlined solution focused on eliminating waste in the approval journey:

  • Process ReengineeringMapped and streamlined end-to-end workflows across all markets, removing redundant approval steps while maintaining necessary controls. Created standardized process that captured required information upfront, eliminating multiple rounds of clarification.
  • Open-Source Technology StackDeployed a modern, open-source platform on top of existing enterprise capabilities that automated routing, notifications, compliance checks and documentation. The system integrated seamlessly with an external HCP payment platform and the internal contract management system, eliminating manual data re-entry and reducing errors.
  • User Experience FocusDesigned interfaces that field teams could navigate intuitively on computer or mobile phone without extensive training. Progressive disclosure ensured users only saw fields relevant to their activity type, while built-in validation caught common errors or potential risks immediately rather than after approval submission.
  • Embedded Compliance & EfficiencyBuilt compliance rules directly into the workflow engine, automatically routing activities to appropriate reviewers based on activity type, budget, and country regulations. Real-time dashboards gave all stakeholders visibility into approval status, eliminating "where is my request" inquiries.
